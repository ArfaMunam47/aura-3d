02 — Product Design

Geometry of Flow

Calibrated contours engineered for tactile presence, structural permanence, and fluid delivery.

01

Machined Cap

Turned from a single billet of aircraft-grade aluminum on a diamond lathe. Precision knurled band provides reliable grip under any condition.

02

Flame-Polished Lip

Contoured neck taper engineered for a completely laminar, drip-free fluid delivery. Drinking feels quiet and natural with every sip.

03

Borosilicate Cylinder

High-purity glass blown to a uniform 2.8mm wall thickness. Thermal-shock resilient from icy cold to boiling teas without stress fractures.

04

Solid Crystal Foot

A generous, solid glass base lowers the center of gravity, firmly anchoring the vessel to desks, nightstands, and stone countertops.

03 — Materials & Detail

Honest Materiality

Every element calibrated for longevity and optical purity. Select a detail below to inspect the physical craftsmanship on the live vessel.

01

Anodized Billet Closure

Diamond-turned from aircraft-grade 6061 aluminum alloy. Micro-knurled circumferential grip band provides positive tactile engagement even with wet hands.

Micro-Knurled Band Laser Monogram Food-Safe Seal
02

Kiln-Fused Silkscreen

The AURA identity is silkscreened with mineral enamel ink directly onto the glass wall and kiln-fired at 620°C, permanently fusing with the borosilicate substrate.

Permanent Mineral Ink Tactile Relief Dishwasher Proof
03

Solid Crystal Punt Foot

A solid 0.4kg crystal glass base with an arched internal punt dome. Anchors the vessel firmly to stone counters and mahogany desks while casting prismatic caustics.

04 — Origin

Formed in Copenhagen

Conceived in our Nordic studio, each vessel undergoes a thirty-stage finishing cycle. Every contour is flame-polished and inspected by hand under calibrated raking light before receiving its batch designation.

Designed to eliminate the constant churn of disposable plastic bottles, AURA was created to be a permanent companion that gains character through decades of daily use.
